# A1: Hoppy, the CodeRabbit hero

Round 4 separates material coordinates from energy coordinates. Generated spheres carry normalized UV2; lens UV2 is a planar disk. The energy shader uses one translated Gaussian reflection highlight. Shield and aura panels use an antialiased hex grid projected in local meters, with 9 cm flat-to-flat spacing. The visual compensates that spacing for roll scaling. The hex pattern no longer multiplies metric texture UVs.

Fur is a noise-warped 64-cycle pattern sampled triplanarly at 6.25 repeats per meter, giving a nominal 2.5 mm streak. Triplanar mapping removes meridian convergence at the crown. Fur albedo varies by less than 2 percent, and the normal strength is reduced. Cloth retains its nominal 1 cm weave. All six cached 256-square ImageTextures have mipmaps. Lathe UVs now use physical circumference per row and cumulative profile arc length, matching the metric planar-face convention.

The hood is a small, flattened, tapered fold against the shoulder volume, with its mouth angled into the skull. Its width is 0.36 m, less than the torso's 0.56 m width; height and depth are each below 0.2 m. The rear mark moves onto the upper back. Eye glass alpha is 0.28, with a clear centre and one moving highlight. Bright untextured CREAM sclera surrounds a NAVY iris ring and dark pupil; pupils retain lane tracking. The nose uses the warm peach-orange inner-ear material.

Skull and cheeks are merged into one surface, leaving one draw call of budget headroom. The capture fixture adds a rear three-quarter `trail` view with run speed 1.0 and asserts that the MATRIX trail is visible. The test that searched this report for a hard-coded host PASS string has been removed. All round-4 improvements were implemented.

Earlier fixes remain: chamfered feet, pocket, frames, bridge and temple arms; duplicated cached SLATE material; uniform articulated roll with attached ears and a zero-blend exit; outward hem stitches; round drawstring aglets; mint frame/hem edge lights; masked brace logos. Capture poses use zero blend and manual animation sampling, and the fixture shuts down AudioService, frees its stage, then drains audio before exit. All animation uses `clock`, with PAUSABLE cosmetic nodes and interpolation disabled.
