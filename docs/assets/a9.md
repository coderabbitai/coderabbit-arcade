# A9: post-processing and screen effects, round 2

All five review blockers are addressed in A9-owned files. No graphical scenes were run, no coordinator PNGs were changed, and nothing was committed or pushed. The existing vignette shader, backend, A7 UI, and shared smoke tests remain untouched.

## Round-2 changes

1. Capture validation now requires nonzero RGB pixel differences between frozen hidden/shown post frames, plus exactly one CanvasItem recursively inside the post layer. The viewport draw counter is labeled `backdrop_3d_draw_calls` and is informational only. It cannot measure the CanvasLayer post draw. A new pure metrics helper is tested with identical images, a one-byte change, null/mismatched images, and nested CanvasItems.
2. Grading strength rose from 0.35 to 0.85, curve authority from 0.32 to 0.85, with stronger navy shadows and mint/cream highlights. A red-dominance mask reduces grade influence by 90 percent on saturated danger reds. Exact black stays black. Analytical headless samples assert visible midtone/navy changes, bounded DANGER-red changes, and source coefficients. The capture adds `a9-grade-off.png` and `a9-grade-on.png`: the same frozen scene/camera with only grading toggled, without grain or streaks confounding the comparison.
3. Grain amplitude rose from 0.004 to 0.018: approximately +/-2.30 8-bit steps on midtones. Deep navy retains at least 75 percent, approximately +/-1.72 steps, while pure black is excluded. The GDScript seed, 24 Hz clock quantization, pause behavior, and monochrome character remain intact. Headless assertions lock amplitude and the navy mask.
4. Streaks use broader spokes (power 6 instead of 26), wider motion segments (power 2 instead of 8), a 35-percent sustained floor, and gain 0.22 instead of 0.055. Nitro raises streak gain another 50 percent. The squared edge mask still protects the central lanes. Headless checks assert the shader coefficients and sample minimum-motion border coverage; host recapture must judge the final look.
5. `DEFAULT_SETTINGS.pursuit` is now false. Other effects stay enabled. Signal handlers retain pursuit state, but strength/corner-bar uniforms remain zero until `set_settings({"pursuit": true})`. The standalone pursuit fixture opts in because it has no legacy HUD. Tests cover default suppression, explicit handover, and restoration of safe defaults between preview views.

All optional review improvements are implemented in these notes. None require a budget or contract exception. `scanlines` is deliberately retired: its compatibility uniform remains at zero and is not animated.

## Asset and integration

`scenes/runner/ui/post_fx.tscn` creates a PAUSABLE CanvasLayer at layer 9 with one full-screen, mouse-ignoring ColorRect. It uses an instance-owned ShaderMaterial, cosmetic interpolation OFF, guarded idempotent `bind(game)`, node-bound tweens, per-effect settings, and a pause-safe `clock`. Rebinding, run boundaries and teardown cancel transient state. The stack includes grading, grain, neutral vignette, damage/tool-hit chromatic separation, intro/stage-clear letterboxing, speed streaks and local Nitro brightness lift. Nitro lift is not spatial bloom. Positive-damage tool hits pulse; resisted tools do not.

The integrator mounts this scene and calls `bind(game)`. Current overlay ownership:

- `runner_hud.gd`, on HUD layer 10, already owns the pursuit red vignette and corner bars through `vignette.gdshader`. Leave A9 pursuit disabled while that owner is active.
- `boss_hud.gd` owns its boss-intro vignette. A9's `boss_intro` handler drives letterboxing only, not another red intro wash. Keep that existing intro vignette unless deliberately redesigning its owner.
- To hand pursuit ownership to A9 later, disable the HUD's pursuit vignette/corner-bar drawing first, then call `post_fx.set_settings({"pursuit": true})`. To hand it back, disable A9 pursuit before restoring the HUD drawing. Enabling this setting does not take ownership of the boss-intro vignette, and A9 never modifies other UI nodes.

Explicit layer audit for integration: `pursuit_alert.tscn`, `boss_hud.tscn`, `stage_banner.tscn`, and `stage_clear.tscn` were reported in the reviewed A7 integration as CanvasLayers with no explicit layer, hence default layer 1. Those versions sit below layer 9 and will be graded, vignetted and letterboxed. Move their integrated CanvasLayers above 9 if they must remain unaffected. This isolated A9 checkout still contains baseline Control roots for those four scenes, so their canvas comes from their parent instead; inspect the A7 versions at merge time. They are A7-owned and were not edited here.

Settings keys: `grade`, `grain`, `vignette`, `pursuit`, `aberration`, `letterbox`, `speed`, `bloom_lift`. `set_settings(overrides)` merges boolean values and immediately gates active effects. All disabled means an unmodified source copy; hide the layer to avoid its draw and screen-read requirement altogether.
