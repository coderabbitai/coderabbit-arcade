## Blocker 1: marker-free interior detail

`shaders/floor_grid.gdshader:54` adds world-space derivative-filtered half-plate joints, beveled joint edges, and corner fasteners with 0.085 m radius. Fasteners include a bright rim, recessed core, and driver slot. These interior details supplement the existing 0.5 × 0.75 m plate boundaries, rather than relying on those few boundary crossings for the detail score.

Normal-map depth increases from 0.5 to **0.9**. Existing 32-texel and larger brush components increase from amplitudes 0.035/0.015 to **0.055/0.025**. No new texture is added. Mipmaps, anisotropic filtering, exact 96 m scroll periodicity, emissive grid lines, and the center seam remain intact.

`environment/hifi/capture_metrics.gd` now measures floor detail in **`Rect2i(780,560,40,50)`**, excluding the x=+1 lane marker. The minimum mean absolute difference from the 3×3 neighbourhood remains **2.0**. A synthetic image with a bright lane beside a flat plate proves the lane cannot carry this score. Other checks lock rivet radius, normal depth, and derivative-filtered sub-seam/rivet shader terms.

## Blocker 2: ceiling structure and depth cue

`environment/hifi/rack_metal.gdshader:65` exempts the masked `detail` emission from `overhead_attenuation`. Tray bars now retain their full light output; only metal fill and rim are attenuated. Overhead fill gain rises from 0.30 to 0.60. Ceiling plane baked gain is 0.36 and grid intensity 0.14, targeting the review's approximately 4–8/255 ceiling mean and 10–25/255 distant-bar visibility. Those are targets, not measured results for this revision.

A new ceiling guard reloads the saved gameplay PNG and requires mean luminance **>= 4/255** in **`Rect2i(480,90,320,50)`**. Deliberately dark ceiling gaps do not inherit the floor's lit-area coverage rule. The capture prints `ASSET A2 CEILING` and exits 1 if its mean fails. Synthetic checks reject a black ceiling, accept a readable ceiling cue, and lock the material settings and unattenuated light-bar detail expression.
