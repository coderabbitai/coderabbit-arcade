## Blocker fixes

1. **Threat tint:** `pursuer_visual.gd:43` drops MultiMesh instance colors and their per-frame uploads. `hifi/plating.gdshader:36` tints albedo from the live threat uniform. Tests verify disabled instance colors, no upload calls, immediate uniform updates, and independent materials between two pursuers.
2. **Emission and aliasing:** `hifi/forge.gd:132` supplies eleven sparse rectangles: three variable-length diff rows, plus/minus marks, one badge, two short seams. `hifi/plating.gdshader:17` filters them with smoothstep/fwidth, reduces glyph weight to 0.18 and restricts emission to front inset faces. Tests measure the union of the actual uploaded rectangles including maximum AA and parallax fringe: **23.12%** of a front face. All three maps now have mipmaps and anisotropic sampling.
3. **UVs:** `hifi/forge.gd:77` emits explicit, normalized per-face UVs, including bevels and corners, at every box size. Cable U wraps its circumference and V runs along its length. Cables use the same cached maps through a separate neutral material with glyphs disabled. Tests check all six faces span [0,1] in both coordinates at card and debris scales, plus cable UV ranges and material selection.
4. **Eyes:** `pursuer_visual.gd:108` seats lenses at y=1.80, z=0.18 within the top row; central mounting cards share z=0.04. `hifi/plating.gdshader:39` adds a hot-core to dark-edge gradient and Fresnel contribution, clamping lens emission to 0.95 even during catch. Tests assert the eye bounds stay below the card top and intersect the top-row front plane, plus shader limits.
5. **Shockwave:** `pursuer_visual.gd:69` uses a 64x24 torus at z=0.72, clear of cards, backings and cables even at maximum pulse scale. `hifi/shockwave.gdshader:6` computes a bright outer leading edge with a soft inward tail. Tests check segmentation, maximum-scale clearance, radius budget, and shader edge/falloff source.
6. **Exhaust:** `pursuer_visual.gd:119` emits 48 additive glyph/streak quads from z=0.48 toward +Z at 1.0 to 1.6 m/s for 0.6 seconds. `hifi/exhaust.gdshader:1` gives each particle an antialiased plus glyph, streak, and lifetime fade. Tests assert front-of-wall origin, direction, travel, dedicated quad/shader and a visibility AABB covering spread, glyph size and complete travel.
7. **Cables:** `hifi/forge.gd:92` routes six capped tendrils around the outer silhouette, in front of the cards. Eight longitudinal sections retain the required 24 radial segments. Cost falls from 5,760 to **2,592 triangles**. Tests require at least 30% of cable vertices beyond x=+/-2.6, front clearance, normalized UVs, and at most 2,700 triangles.

All optional improvements are implemented. Opaque shaders use back-face culling; geometry explicitly follows Godot's clockwise front-face convention with outward normals, tested triangle by triangle. Each shared chamfer is emitted once, eliminating duplicate coplanar strips. Six bevelled, nonemissive headline backing plates are combined into one draw. DANGER selection uses `HEADLINES.find("CHANGES REQUESTED")`. No improvement was rejected for a budget or contract conflict.
