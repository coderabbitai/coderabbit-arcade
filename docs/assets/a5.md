## Blocker 1: a reactor with physical mass

`boss_art.gd:154` replaces the stock dark sphere, thin torus collars and floating red quad with a single moving reactor assembly:

- An ArrayMesh housing combines a chamfered annular body and eight bevelled armour petals. Its approximately 1.48 m diameter and PLATING material give the centre substantial lit metal area. It shares the existing cached albedo, Sobel normal and roughness maps.
- Eight MATRIX vent slots sit in the gaps between petals, recessed at Z=0.12 behind front armour at Z=0.30. Their combined mesh uses the existing hue-preserving luminous path.
- Two containment bands have 0.12 m radial width and 0.14 m depth. Each sweeps an eight-sided chamfered section over 24 angular segments. Both bands share one surface and draw.
- A generated 24×12 segmented spherical heart uses `hifi/reactor.gdshader`: a masked equatorial energy belt, meridian seams and a view-dependent fresnel shell contribution, with clock-driven modulation and emission capped at 0.6. There is no stock SphereMesh. The old red text quad is removed.
- The transient Swarm phase/attack ring also uses a forged 0.12 m section band, so no stock TorusMesh remains anywhere in this boss scene.

`tests/assets/a5_check.gd:459` asserts ArrayMesh housing/heart, housing bounds at least 1 m across, PLATING albedo, vent depth and MATRIX accent, substantial band section, masked/fresnel shader structure, and absence of SphereMesh/TorusMesh nodes. The existing motion/hook sweep now enforces **12 draws and 22,700 triangles specifically for the Swarm**, including attack, intro, phase and outro poses. Final peak is **11 draws and 22,080 triangles**.

## Improvements

- A sleeve/socket collar caps the dormant fourth-neck stump. A matching open shoulder vent on the left mounts directly onto the torso. Both reuse the neck-sleeve MultiMesh. Existing connected-neck and monotone-taper assertions remain intact.
- The inter-neck LILAC arc now sits inside a dark bevelled channel. Its backing and raised lips share one surface; the entire assembly follows seam recoil together. Tests assert that channel lips project beyond the emissive strip.
- Lane fill fades to zero over the final 10% of UV.y. Tests verify the fade expression is part of the lane mask, with half strength at 0.95 and zero at 1.0. The centre fill remains nonzero at half countdown.
- Removed unused `Forge.ellipse_ring()` and the tracer's unused Materials preload. The damage-number cursor now cycles all six reusable slots while `limit_numbers()` preserves the maximum of three active numbers. Tests exercise all six slots and the draw cap.
- Added `Forge.clear_cache()` and explicit cleanup in both the A5 check and capture fixture. Verbose diagnosis showed the lingering two ObjectDB instances were **AudioStreamPlaybackMP3 and AudioStreamMP3**, with `res://Assets/Audio/happy_adveture.mp3` still in use, rather than a remaining Forge resource. Fixtures now call the existing AudioService shutdown hook before waiting briefly and quitting. The final A5 check and headless capture rejection have **no ObjectDB/resource-leak warnings**. No audio/backend source was changed. Graphical shutdown still requires host verification.

All round-5 improvements were implemented within budget. All nine hooks, `play_intro(seconds)`, pause behavior and existing envelope limits remain intact. Earlier round-4 metal arcs, tile-only silhouette, lane fill, connected necks and limited-range accent lights remain covered.
