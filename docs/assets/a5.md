## Blocker 1: a reactor with physical mass

`boss_art.gd:154` replaces the stock dark sphere, thin torus collars and floating red quad with a single moving reactor assembly:

- An ArrayMesh housing combines a chamfered annular body and eight bevelled armour petals. Its approximately 1.48 m diameter and PLATING material give the centre substantial lit metal area. It shares the existing cached albedo, Sobel normal and roughness maps.
- Eight MATRIX vent slots sit in the gaps between petals, recessed at Z=0.12 behind front armour at Z=0.30. Their combined mesh uses the existing hue-preserving luminous path.
- Two containment bands have 0.12 m radial width and 0.14 m depth. Each sweeps an eight-sided chamfered section over 24 angular segments. Both bands share one surface and draw.
- A generated 24×12 segmented spherical heart uses `hifi/reactor.gdshader`: a masked equatorial energy belt, meridian seams and a view-dependent fresnel shell contribution, with clock-driven modulation and emission capped at 0.6. There is no stock SphereMesh. The old red text quad is removed.
- The transient Swarm phase/attack ring also uses a forged 0.12 m section band, so no stock TorusMesh remains anywhere in this boss scene.

`tests/assets/a5_check.gd:459` asserts ArrayMesh housing/heart, housing bounds at least 1 m across, PLATING albedo, vent depth and MATRIX accent, substantial band section, masked/fresnel shader structure, and absence of SphereMesh/TorusMesh nodes. The existing motion/hook sweep now enforces **12 draws and 22,700 triangles specifically for the Swarm**, including attack, intro, phase and outro poses. Final peak is **11 draws and 22,080 triangles**.

## C4: signature attack visuals (contract 2.4), 2026-09-28

The C3 mechanics (boss_attacks.gd, contract 2.4 §11.4-§11.6) now have a look per boss. Mechanics,
tuning and the validator are untouched; everything reads the documented API (boss_attack_planned /
telegraph / committed, play_commit, the projectile's `signature_variants`, `motion` and `age`).

**Telegraphs** (`boss/boss_fx.gd`, `boss/telegraph.gd`, new `boss/visuals/boss_telegraph_marks.gd`).
Each telegraph opens an attack record (a preallocated pool of 8) carrying the attack that
boss_attack_planned announced. The three lane slots keep DANGER red as the base meaning, but each
slot now holds up to four countdowns and shows the one that fires first, so a branching volley, a
flaky beat's candidates or an overlapping slow wall never overwrite each other. The per-boss accent
is one additive MultiMesh (256 preallocated floor quads, one draw) rewritten every frame from the
records:

| Boss / signature | Floor mode |
|---|---|
| Swarm flaky | MATRIX glitch tiles on both candidates; the showing candidate alternates at `swap_seconds` and the other candidate's red lane dims to 18 %; at the commit the tiles SNAP into one solid column with lit edges and the losing lane's telegraph is removed |
| Swarm scatter | tiles down the middle, a diamond at the split z and a MATRIX fork into the two outer lanes, which are marked as the impact lanes |
| Hydra branching | one LILAC branch per head grown from the trunk in firing order, then down its lane, numbered by head+1 chevrons |

| Hydra force push | a pulsing merge bar and chevrons shoved down the two lanes |
| Monolith quake | an ORANGE ring expanding from the boss's floor and fissures racing the lanes |
| Monolith legacy wall | ORANGE fissures where slabs stand; the gap and its shift target in MINT from the first frame, with an arrow at the shift z |
| Monolith debris | a shadow per slab at its landing spot, growing until the telegraph fires, with a dashed drop line |

**Projectiles** (new `boss/visuals/boss_projectile_art.gd`, `hazards/visuals/projectile_visual.gd`
now declares `signature_variants`). Swarm orbs are PASS/FAIL test-screen tiles (flaky ones stutter

seam and each half keeps its side at the split (the twin is hidden until then); Hydra orbs are jawed
head bolts; force_push is a shoved merge block with the conflict glyph; sweep is a lateral serpent

by `warm()` when BossFX mounts; spawns only add nodes, never resources, and never mutate a shared
resource. Everything is posed from the shot's `age`, so pause freezes it.
